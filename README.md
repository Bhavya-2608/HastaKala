# Hasta Kala 🎨
### Artisan's Digital Workspace & Inventory Manager

Hasta Kala is a modern Android application designed specifically for artisans and handicraft sellers. It provides a robust platform to manage product inventory, track sales variations, and analyze business growth through data-driven insights.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Multi-User Support
- **Personalized Workspace**: Each artisan has a unique account. Data (inventory, sales, and settings) is strictly isolated and unique to the logged-in user email.
- **Secure Auth**: Local authentication with support for profile management and account deletion.

### 📦 Advanced Inventory Management
- **Multi-Variation Support**: Add products with multiple color variations in a single step.
- **Dynamic Pricing**: Manage and update product prices globally or per item.
- **Comprehensive Editing**: Rename products, add new colors to existing stock, or remove specific variations without losing history.
- **Smart Alerts**: Visual indicators and dedicated "Restock" screen for items with low quantity.

### 🧾 Quick Bill & Sales
- **Intuitive Billing**: Rapidly generate bills by selecting products and colors directly from active inventory.
- **Stock Integration**: Sales automatically reduce inventory levels in real-time.
- **Income Log**: Detailed transaction history with date-wise records.

### 📊 Business Analytics
- **Sales Stats**: View best-selling products and color distributions.
- **Revenue Trends**: Analyze daily, weekly, and monthly income through visual summaries.
- **Growth Tracking**: Percentage comparisons of today's sales vs. yesterday.

### 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Language**: Kotlin
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose (Modern, declarative UI)
- **Database**: Room (Local persistent storage with SQLite)
- **Architecture**: MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel)
- **Navigation**: Navigation Compose
- **Asynchronous**: Kotlin Coroutines & Flow (Reactive data streams)

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Clone the repository**
2. **Open in Android Studio**:
   - Open the project folder.
   - Wait for Gradle sync to complete.
3. **Run the App**:
   - Select an emulator or physical device (API 24+).
   - Click the 'Run' icon.

## 📱 Screen Overview
- **Welcome/Auth**: Secure login and artisan registration.
- **Dashboard**: High-level business overview and quick actions.
- **Inventory**: Detailed product list with search and filtering.
- **Quick Bill**: Interactive shopping cart for customer transactions.
- **Settings**: Theme customization (Dark/Light), profile updates, and data management.

## 🛡️ Privacy & Security
Hasta Kala respects artisan privacy. All data is stored locally on the device using a Room database, ensuring that business records remain private and accessible even offline.
